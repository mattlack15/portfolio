Research and decisions

The useful lesson from the design criticism is to give each layout a purpose. Repeated cards, default typography, gradients, and decorative charts can make unrelated websites converge on the same structure. These choices instead give visitors four different ways to browse the actual work.

Design

The revised first design keeps its original layout and uses plain typography. Workbench prioritizes inspection. Gallery prioritizes imagery. Personal Index prioritizes scanning and search. Screenshots and board photographs come from your backend; there are no invented charts, testimonials, or project results.

Writing

Language-model research documents vocabulary overrepresentation, while simple-writing advice favors concrete, ordinary language. The edited summaries say what each project does and what you worked on. They avoid broad expertise claims and describe unfinished work accurately.

These are editing guidelines, not reliable tests of authorship. The full technical writeups retain your original content, with heading levels adjusted to fit the page structure.
